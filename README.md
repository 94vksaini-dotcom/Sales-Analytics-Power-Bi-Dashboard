# 📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes sales performance for a mobile and consumer electronics market. The dashboard provides insights into sales trends, dealer performance, product contribution, promotional schemes, and competitor comparison. The analysis helps business teams understand sales performance and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

- Power BI
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Sales Data Analysis

---

# 1️⃣ Sales Overview Dashboard

## 🎯 Dashboard Objective

The Sales Overview dashboard provides a high-level summary of overall sales performance including revenue, units sold, target achievement, and sales growth trends.

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

**Total Sales**  
Total revenue generated from product sales.

**Total Units**  
Total number of products sold.

**Target Achievement %**  
Shows how much of the sales target has been achieved.

**Total Sales Gap**  
Difference between sales target and actual sales.

**Sales Growth %**  
Percentage growth in sales compared to the previous period.

---

## 📈 Visualizations Used

**Monthly Sales Trend**  
Shows sales performance over time and identifies seasonal sales patterns.

**Area Wise Sales**  
Displays sales contribution from different regions of Delhi.

**Top Models**  
Highlights the top-selling product models based on revenue.

---

## 🔎 Business Insights

- South Delhi and West Delhi contribute the highest sales revenue.
- Certain models such as **V29** and **A14** generate the highest revenue.
- Monthly sales show fluctuations indicating seasonal demand patterns.
- Target achievement above **100%** indicates strong sales performance.

---

# 2️⃣ Dealer Performance Dashboard

## 🎯 Dashboard Objective

This dashboard evaluates dealer performance and identifies top and low-performing dealers based on their sales contribution.

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

**Total Dealers**  
Total number of dealers participating in sales.

**Dealer Sales**  
Total sales generated by all dealers.

**Total Target**  
Combined sales targets assigned to dealers.

**Over Achievement**  
Sales exceeding the assigned targets.

**Dealer Rank**  
Ranking of dealers based on their sales performance.

**Average Dealer Sales**  
Average sales generated per dealer.

---

## 📈 Visualizations Used

**Top 10 Dealers**  
Shows the highest-performing dealers based on sales.

**Bottom 5 Dealers**  
Identifies underperforming dealers.

**Dealer Contribution %**  
Displays the share of each dealer in total sales.

**Sales Summary Table**  
Detailed dealer-wise sales data including units sold, sales amount, and target achievement.

---

## 🔎 Business Insights

- A small group of dealers contributes a large portion of total revenue.
- Top dealers significantly outperform average dealers.
- Bottom dealers may require additional support or strategy changes.
- Dealer ranking helps identify potential growth opportunities.

---

# 3️⃣ Product Performance Dashboard

## 🎯 Dashboard Objective

This dashboard analyzes product-level performance to identify top-selling brands and product contribution to total sales.

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

**Total Sales**  
Total revenue generated from all products.

**Units Sold**  
Total number of units sold across all brands.

**Total Profit**  
Profit generated from product sales.

**Average Cost Price**  
Average cost price of products.

---

## 📈 Visualizations Used

**Top 3 Products**  
Shows the brands generating the highest sales revenue.

**Product Contribution %**  
Displays the share of each brand in total sales.

**Product Sales Trend**  
Monthly sales trends for different brands.

**Fast Moving vs Slow Moving Products**  
Identifies products with high and low sales velocity.

---

## 🔎 Business Insights

- **Xiaomi** and **Realme** contribute the highest sales revenue.
- Certain brands consistently show strong monthly sales performance.
- Fast-moving products generate the majority of revenue.
- Slow-moving products may require promotional strategies.

---

# 4️⃣ Scheme Analysis Dashboard

## 🎯 Dashboard Objective

This dashboard analyzes the effectiveness of promotional schemes and their impact on sales performance.

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

**Scheme Sales**  
Total sales generated during promotional schemes.

**No Scheme Sales**  
Sales generated without promotional offers.

**Scheme Impact**  
Additional revenue generated due to schemes.

**Scheme Impact %**  
Percentage impact of schemes on total sales.

---

## 📈 Visualizations Used

**Product vs Scheme Sales**  
Shows which brands perform better during schemes.

**Model vs Scheme Sales**  
Highlights models that benefit the most from promotions.

**Monthly Scheme vs No Scheme Sales**  
Compares sales performance with and without promotional offers.

**Scheme Summary Table**  
Detailed model-level scheme performance.

---

## 🔎 Business Insights

- Promotional schemes significantly increase product sales.
- Certain models respond better to discount campaigns.
- Scheme-driven sales contribute a major portion of total revenue.
- Monthly analysis helps identify the best time for promotions.

---

# 5️⃣ Competitor Analysis Dashboard

## 🎯 Dashboard Objective

This dashboard compares company sales with competitor performance and evaluates market positioning.

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

**Total Sales**  
Total company sales revenue.

**Competitor Total Sales**  
Estimated competitor sales revenue.

**Sales Gap**  
Difference between company sales and competitor sales.

**Market Share %**  
Company's share in the overall market.

---

## 📈 Visualizations Used

**Total Sales Trend Comparison**  
Comparison of company sales vs competitor sales over time.

**Price Comparison**  
Compares product prices with competitors across brands.

**Competitor Summary Table**  
Model-wise comparison of unit price, competitor price, and sales performance.

---

## 🔎 Business Insights

- Competitor sales are slightly higher than company sales.
- Price comparison reveals competitive pricing differences.
- Some models have a strong competitive advantage.
- Understanding competitor pricing helps improve market strategies.

---

# ✅ Conclusion

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of sales performance including dealer contribution, product trends, promotional schemes, and competitor comparison.

The insights from this dashboard help businesses make better strategic decisions, optimize pricing strategies, and improve overall sales performance.
